# Lineage

Canonical source: `berlin_trip_subway_3sid_v6_0_0_music_rebuild.zip`.

Despite its archive name, the included source identifies itself as the
`U83R RUL3Z` 3SID timing-locked C64 megademo. It is therefore catalogued under
its implementation identity rather than the misleading archive filename.

The adjacent `mega/` folder is not imported: its `src/subway.s` is JSON data,
not 6502 assembly, and its checksum does not match the archive manifest.
Build products are excluded.
